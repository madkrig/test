How the Process Layer and SOP Layer interlink

The Process Layer defines what happens and in what order — the business activities grouped into end-to-end processes. The SOP Layer defines how each activity is actually carried out — the documented, repeatable procedures. They aren't a strict one-to-one hierarchy: a single SOP is often written once and reused by several process steps, which is what makes the two layers a network rather than a tree.
